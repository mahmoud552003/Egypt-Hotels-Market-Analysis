# 🏨 Egypt Hotels Market Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the hotel market across Egypt to uncover insights into hotel pricing, ratings, distribution, and competition between cities.

The analysis aims to understand market trends, identify pricing patterns, and generate meaningful insights that can support data-driven investment decisions.

This project was completed as a graduation project during the **National Telecommunication Institute (NTI) Data Analytics Training Program**.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze hotel distribution across Egyptian cities.
- Explore the relationship between hotel ratings and prices.
- Investigate how distance from city centers relates to hotel pricing.
- Compare hotel markets across different cities.
- Identify potential investment opportunities through data analysis.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

- **Total Hotels:** 1,040
- **Geographical Coverage:** 17 Egyptian cities
- **Data Focus:** Hotel prices, ratings, locations, and market competition.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Data analysis and preprocessing.
- **Pandas:** Data manipulation and cleaning.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Higher hotel ratings do not always mean higher prices.
- No clear relationship was found between hotel distance from the city center and its price.
- Cairo has one of the highest numbers of hotels, reflecting a competitive hotel market.
- El Alamein recorded the highest average hotel prices, indicating a premium market with potential revenue opportunities.
